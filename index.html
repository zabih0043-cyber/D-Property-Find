How It Works

Three steps. No surprises.

01

Tell me what you need

We chat over WhatsApp, phone, or email. You share your budget, area preferences, must-haves, and timeline. The first conversation is free.

02

I do the searching

I scout listings across every channel, visit shortlisted properties in person, vet landlords or sellers, and send you a curated shortlist with my honest opinion on each.

03

You move in (or move on)

When we find the right place, I help you negotiate, handle the paperwork, and make sure the transition is smooth. Search fee paid upfront; remainder paid on a successful match.
