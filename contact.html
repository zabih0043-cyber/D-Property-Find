Quick Questions

Things people ask before getting in touch.

How much does it cost?
Fees vary by what you're looking for — rental searches are different from purchase searches. There's a search fee upfront and a balance paid on a successful match. We agree the full amount in our first conversation, before any work begins, so there are no surprises.
What if you don't find me anything?
If I don't find you the right place within the agreed timeframe, you don't pay the success portion. The upfront search fee covers my time and effort up to that point.
Do you work with international clients who haven't arrived yet?
Yes — regularly. I view properties on your behalf, send video walk-throughs, and can secure a place before you land. Just mention this when you reach out.
How long does a typical search take?
It depends on what you're looking for and how specific. Rentals usually 2–6 weeks. Purchases longer, often 2–3 months. We agree on a timeline at the start.
Can I see properties in person before deciding?
Of course. I'll arrange viewings for you (if you're in Cape Town) or send detailed video and notes (if you're not).
