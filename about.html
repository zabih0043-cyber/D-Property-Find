About

Hi, I'm Doniel.

I started D Property Find because Cape Town's rental market is hard to navigate alone.

Doniel's story

I started D Property Find after watching friends and family lose weeks of their lives to property searches — viewing places that misrepresented themselves online, dealing with unresponsive agents, and signing leases under pressure. I knew there was a better way.

I work the way I'd want someone to work for me. Honest opinions — including telling you when a place I've found isn't quite right. No upselling, no rushing, no pressure. Your timeline is your timeline.

I live in Cape Town. I know the suburbs — the quiet streets in Tamboerskloof, the family pockets in Constantia, the buzz of Sea Point, the warmth of Observatory. Local knowledge isn't a sales pitch; it's how I do my job.

When I'm not viewing properties, you'll find me at the Old Biscuit Mill on a Saturday or somewhere on the coastal path between Sea Point and Camps Bay. Cape Town is home.

How I Work

Three things you can count on.

Honest opinions, always.

I'll tell you if a place isn't right, even after I've found it. You're paying me to be straight with you.

No pressure.

I work at your pace. Some clients move in a week, others take three months. Both are completely fine.

Hands and feet on the ground.

I view every property in person before I send it to you. Video, photos, and my honest first impression.
